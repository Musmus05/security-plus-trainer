## Exam integrity — no brain dumps

CompTIA's _Authorized Materials Use Policy_ states that CompTIA does not authorize, endorse, or
condone the use of content from unauthorized third-party training sites — commonly called "brain
dumps" — and that individuals who use such materials in preparation for a CompTIA exam may have
their certifications revoked and be suspended from future testing under the CompTIA Candidate
Agreement.

This project takes that seriously. Accordingly:

1. **No real exam question is reproduced here**, in whole, in part, or paraphrased from recalled
   exam content.
2. Every practice question is **authored from the published objectives**, testing the same concept
   without imitating any actual item.
3. Contributions that appear to be sourced from recalled or leaked exam content **must be rejected
   in review**, regardless of how well they are written.

If you believe any content in this repository violates the policy above, open an issue and it will
be removed.

## Your data

The application stores your progress in your browser's `localStorage`. Nothing is transmitted to a
server — there is no backend and no analytics. Clearing your browser data clears your progress,
which is why the app provides a JSON export in its settings.
